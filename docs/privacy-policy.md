# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-27_

Dark Theme for Play Console is a Chrome extension that switches on a dark mode for the Google Play
Console. This policy describes what it can access and what it does with it. It is short because the
extension does very little.

## The extension collects nothing

There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. The extension makes no network
requests of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

## What it can access, and why

- **Play Console pages** at `play.google.com/console`, and no other site. It adds a class to the
  page that switches on the console's own dark colours, and loads a style sheet. To tell which
  charts are drawn on a white background, it reads the colour of a single pixel of each chart. It
  never reads, stores or sends the page's text, your app data or anything you type.
- **Storage**, to remember one setting: whether the dark mode is on or off.
- **The toolbar menu**, to add two tip links to the icon's right-click menu. Choosing one opens Buy
  Me a Coffee or PayPal in a new tab.

## The things that happen outside the extension

1. **Chrome sync.** The on or off setting is saved with Chrome's sync storage. If you sign in to
   Chrome with sync turned on, Google keeps that one setting with your Google account.
2. **The Chrome Web Store.** Installing and updating the extension is a transaction between your
   browser and Google, with its own privacy policy.
3. **A tip, if you choose to leave one.** Buy Me a Coffee and PayPal handle the payment under their
   own privacy policies.
4. **An email you choose to send.** It reaches the support mailbox and is used only to answer you.

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes its setting from your browser.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the history of this file is public
